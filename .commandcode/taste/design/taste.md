# Taste — design / UI

- Wants the landing page design language modeled on cursor.com; explicitly asks to identify and copy its sections exactly. Confidence: 0.8
- Mobile view must be deliberately optimized (proportionate hero copy and buttons, borders removed on mobile). Confidence: 0.85
- Prefers glass/liquid-glass cards over solid filled cards, on an off-white background that shows through the cards; dislikes card shadows and dark CTA backgrounds. Confidence: 0.8
- CTA buttons should share one consistent hover treatment across the site (the hero's effect, "popping out of the screen"), while the header CTA should stay subtler (no pop-out). Confidence: 0.8
- Wants consistent, natural spacing between sections and no visible seams between adjacent sections. Confidence: 0.75
- Cards/mocks within a section should be the same size, and interacting with a mock must not change its size. Confidence: 0.8
- Hero and CTA copy must be very clear and match the flow/narrative of the landing page; avoids em dashes in copy. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers to specify exact hex color tokens for card/UI styling rather than approximate shades. Confidence: 0.6
- Sections should have meaningful graphics (cursor.com sections "always have graphics"), and graphics should match the section's color language. Confidence: 0.6
- Footer should always sit at the bottom of every page. Confidence: 0.55
- Claim-heavy sections should link to the actual source documents (e.g., law documents). Confidence: 0.55
