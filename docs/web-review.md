## 1. Solution — complete the three-layer thesis

The current third layer, **Traceability**, does not fully express the intended communication benefit. Ensure the section communicates all three layers:

- **Visibility** — every party sees their own obligation and the interlocking statutory clocks in one place.
- **Execution assistance** — actively helps users meet the legal specificity standard rather than only tracking deadlines.
- **Communication** — evidence and responses move on-platform instead of through email, post, and phone.

This section should answer: “Isn’t this just a dashboard?”

- Show the flow from consultation letter to sent response.
- Show deadline oversight across the whole caseload, using calendar days rather than working days.
- Show evidence suitable for governor packs and local-authority moderation, including response rates, needs met by category, and capacity strain.

- SEND Regulations 2014
- SEND Code of Practice 2015
