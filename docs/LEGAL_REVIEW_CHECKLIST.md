# Legal Review Checklist

This internal checklist records information that the founders and legal advisers must confirm before Unisen publishes final legal documents or processes live SEND case data. It is not a public-facing notice and must not be linked from the marketing website.

## Organisation and accountability

- [ ] Confirm the final legal entity name.
- [ ] Add the company number after incorporation.
- [ ] Confirm the registered address and correspondence address.
- [ ] Confirm the data-controller identity for the website, pilots and future product.
- [ ] Decide whether to appoint a Data Protection Officer and confirm the privacy contact.
- [ ] Assess ICO registration and data protection fee requirements.

## Lawful processing and children's information

- [ ] Confirm the Article 6 lawful basis for each website enquiry purpose.
- [ ] Confirm the future Article 9 conditions for SEND, disability and health data.
- [ ] Complete and approve a Data Protection Impact Assessment before processing live SEND case data.
- [ ] Prepare age-appropriate children's privacy information.
- [ ] Map controller, joint-controller and processor roles for families, schools, trusts, local authorities, healthcare professionals, specialists and Unisen.

## Contracts and notices

- [ ] Prepare a customer data processing agreement.
- [ ] Prepare a pilot or subscription agreement.
- [ ] Prepare family user terms.
- [ ] Prepare the product privacy notice before real SEND case data is processed.
- [ ] Review the website privacy notice and website terms with qualified legal advisers.

## Providers, subprocessors and transfers

- [ ] Publish and maintain a confirmed subprocessor list.
- [ ] Confirm international transfer locations and applicable safeguards for every provider.
- [ ] Confirm Convex processing locations, contractual terms and retention behaviour.
- [ ] Confirm Resend processing locations, contractual terms and retention behaviour.
- [ ] Define retention for the team's email inbox and related business correspondence.
- [ ] Confirm Cloudflare Turnstile processing, cookies or similar technologies, transfer position and retention.

## Retention and security

- [ ] Approve a product data-retention schedule covering case data, documents, audit information, exports, backups and deletion.
- [ ] Confirm the website enquiry database deletion process and whether 30 days remains appropriate.
- [ ] Validate every public security claim against implemented controls and evidence.
- [ ] Define incident response, access review and deletion responsibilities before a pilot involving personal data.
